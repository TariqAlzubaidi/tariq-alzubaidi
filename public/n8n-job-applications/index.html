Tariq Local Job Applications

Tariq Local Job Applications is a private, owner-operated job search and application assistant for Tariq Al-Zubaidi. It searches public HR and administrative vacancies in Jeddah, checks dates and application instructions, prepares factual applications from the owner's approved resume, prevents duplicate applications, and records outcomes in the owner's Google Sheets tracker. The local service is restricted to the owner's computer and is not offered as a public sign-in service. Contact: ta.alzubaidi@gmail.com.
