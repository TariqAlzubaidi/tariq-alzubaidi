01 / Project Overview

This self-directed portfolio project uses a synthetic workforce dataset to demonstrate how HR data can be organized, summarized, and communicated through an Excel dashboard. It covers eight departments and five work locations.

Data transparency: All 500 employee records are fictional and used exclusively for learning and portfolio demonstration. The findings describe this sample dataset, not an actual organization.

02 / Business Questions

  • How is headcount distributed across departments and locations?
  • How do average salaries compare between departments?
  • How do performance ratings vary by department?
  • What is the distribution of employment status and gender?

03 / Tools & Approach

Microsoft ExcelPivotTablesPivotChartsKPI Dashboard

Organized employee-level records, summarized key measures by category, and presented comparisons using PivotTables, charts, and dashboard KPI cards. The workbook includes dedicated worksheets for headcount, salary, performance, employment status, gender, and location analysis.

05 / Key Findings

  1. Operations accounts for the largest workforce share: 105 of 500 employees (21%).
  2. Information Technology has the highest average basic salary: 12,556.92 SAR, compared with the overall average of 10,726 SAR.
  3. Human Resources has the highest average performance rating: 3.60 out of 5, compared with 3.44 overall.
  4. Jeddah is the largest work location: 228 employees (45.6%).
  5. Employment records are predominantly Active: 431 employees (86.2%); 48 are classified Resigned (9.6%) and 21 On Leave (4.2%).

06 / HR Recommendations & Limitations

  • Review workload and staffing demand in Operations before recommending headcount changes.
  • Use job grade, experience, and role-level data to interpret salary differences fairly; department averages alone do not establish pay inequity.
  • Investigate the criteria and distribution behind performance scores before comparing departmental effectiveness.
  • Consider Jeddah's larger employee base when prioritizing HR support, while validating needs against operational demand.
  • Track resignations over a defined period with average workforce size before calculating a formal turnover rate.

Analytical limitation: This is descriptive analysis of a synthetic snapshot. The dataset does not establish causation, operational efficiency, or annual turnover rates.
